# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 22 October 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how NewGen Apps ("we", "us") handles information in the Curio Android app ("Curio", "the app"). Curio has no user accounts, and we do not run a server that stores your personal data.

## 1. Information stored on your device

Curio saves the following only on your device (using Android local storage), so the app works the way you set it up:

- Your onboarding answers and preferences (topics you follow or mute, reminder times, daily goal, fact styles, sound and notification settings).
- Your activity inside the app: favorites, collections, quiz scores, streaks, rewards, unlocked share themes and premium status.

We do not receive this data. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. If Android Auto Backup is enabled on your device, Google may back this data up to your Google account under Google's own terms.

## 2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or microphone access, and we do not require sign-in.

## 3. Content downloaded from the internet

To keep facts and quizzes fresh, Curio downloads content files (facts and quiz questions) from public GitHub repositories. Like any internet request, this exposes your IP address and basic request details to GitHub, which is governed by [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). No personal information from the app is sent in these requests.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Curio shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, and may show personalized ads depending on your device settings and consent choices. We do not control this data collection. See [Google's advertising policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android: *Settings > Privacy > Ads* (the path varies by device). Premium members may see fewer or no ads as described in the app.

## 5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Premium membership purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment card details. Google may share purchase status with us so the app can unlock premium features. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Curio schedules local reminders on your device at the times you choose. You can turn them off anytime in the app or in Android system settings.

## 7. Sharing

When you use the share feature, Curio creates an image (a share card) and hands it to the app you pick, such as a messaging app. Where it goes after that is controlled by you and that app. We do not sell your personal information.

## 8. Children

Curio is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help resolve it.

## 9. Security

Your app data stays on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Your choices

- Clear or uninstall the app to delete locally stored data.
- Manage ad personalization and advertising ID in your Android settings.
- Manage notification permission in Android settings.

## 11. Third-party content and attribution

Some quiz questions are adapted from the [Open Trivia Database](https://opentdb.com) under the CC BY-SA 4.0 license and curated by the Curio Editorial Team.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page with its "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
