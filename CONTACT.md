# Contact Us

We usually reply within 2-3 business days.

**Email:** [YOUR_EMAIL@example.com](mailto:YOUR_EMAIL@example.com)

## Wrong or outdated fact?

Tell us the fact or question, the category, and what you think is correct, ideally with a source. You can also open an issue at [github.com/hi2dineshnow/facts-and-quiz](https://github.com/hi2dineshnow/facts-and-quiz/issues).

## What to include for support

- Your phone model and Android version
- The Curio version (shown in Google Play)
- A short description of the problem, with a screenshot if possible

## Purchases and refunds

Premium purchases are handled by Google Play. For billing, cancellations and refunds, use *Google Play Store > Payments & subscriptions*. For premium features not unlocking, use "Restore purchases" in the app first, then email us.

## Privacy requests

Curio stores your data on your device only, so you can delete it by clearing app data or uninstalling. For any privacy question, see the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) or email us.
